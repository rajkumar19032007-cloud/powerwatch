Transparent Statistical Modeling Methodology

PowerWatch generates transparent statistical forecasts using Seasonal Exponential Smoothing (Holt-Winters Diurnal Decomposition) combined with academic calendar weighting. The algorithm calculates moving baselines, day-of-week multipliers (accounting for weekend drops in academic blocks and surges in hostels), and thermal ambient load adjustments rather than obscure black-box claims.
